Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This application is developed for personal use. It automates the publishing of the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Information Collected

The application requests authorization through TikTok OAuth. Upon authorization, the application only stores:

  • TikTok access token
  • TikTok refresh token

These credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never transmitted anywhere other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

How Information Is Used

The stored tokens are used solely to authenticate API requests and to publish videos to the owner's TikTok account. The application does not collect, process, or store any information about other TikTok users.

Data Sharing

  • No personal information is sold or shared.
  • No analytics services are used.
  • No advertising services are used.
  • No third parties have access to user information.

Publishing Behavior

Uploaded videos are published only after explicit authorization by the account owner. The application does not publish content automatically without the owner initiating the action.

Data Retention & Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's account settings (Settings > Manage account > Apps and websites). Once revoked, the locally stored tokens are no longer valid and can be deleted from the owner's computer.
